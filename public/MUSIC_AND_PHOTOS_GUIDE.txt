HOW TO CHANGE MUSIC AND ADD PHOTOS:
====================================

1. HOW TO CHANGE THE SONG:
-------------------------
You have two super easy ways:

Way A (Directly on the website - Instant):
- Click the music player / sliders icon (🎵) in the top-right corner of the website.
- Click "Choose Audio File From Device" and select any MP3, M4A, or WAV song file from your phone or computer.
- It will instantly start playing and automatically save to your browser!
- You can also paste any direct audio URL or adjust the volume.

Way B (In the project files - Permanent):
- Put your favorite song file named `song.mp3` right into this `/public/` folder (`/public/song.mp3`).
- The website will automatically detect and play `/public/song.mp3` as the background music!

2. HOW TO ADD PHOTOS OF HIM:
---------------------------
Way A (Directly on the website - Instant):
- On Screen 3 ("I LOVE YOU MORE"): Tap "Put His Photo Here 📸" to put his picture right on the hero screen.
- On Screen 6 (The Birthday Letter): Scroll down to "Our Memory Gallery 📸💕" and tap "Add His Photo 📸".
- You can upload photos from your phone camera or computer.
- You can click the pencil icon to write customized romantic captions for each picture (e.g. "My favorite smile", "My Batman 🦇💗").
- Photos are saved so they stay there whenever you view the site!

Way B (In the project files):
- Put image files in `/public/images/` or in `/src/assets/images/`.
