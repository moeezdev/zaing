# 🎵 HOW TO ADD YOUR SONG TO THIS FOLDER:
=========================================

Simply drop your MP3, M4A, or WAV audio file directly into this folder (/public/song/)!

Recommended names:
- song.mp3
- music.mp3
- birthday.mp3

When you put your song file here (e.g. /public/song/song.mp3), the website will automatically detect it and use it as your romantic background song on loop!

You can also change or upload songs directly on the live website anytime by clicking the music sliders icon (🎛️) in the top-right corner.
